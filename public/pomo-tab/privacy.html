Pomo Tab Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Pomo Tab is a Chrome extension that replaces the new tab page with a clock, weather, prayer times, today's calendar and focus sounds, and adds a Pomodoro timer page that shows your Todoist tasks.

In short: everything Pomo Tab stores stays in your own browser. The developer does not run a server, does not collect analytics, and never receives your data. The extension only talks directly to the services listed below, to show you your own information.

What is stored, and where

Pomo Tab saves its settings and data in Chrome's local extension storage on your device. This includes your timer settings and history of focus time, your Todoist API token, your Google sign-in access token and email address, calendar addresses you enter, your weather and prayer location setting, and recent copies of your tasks, events, weather and prayer times so pages load quickly. None of this is synced or sent to the developer. Removing the extension deletes it.

Each of these services handles requests under its own privacy policy. Location is only used if you allow it when Chrome asks, or if you type a city in settings.

What Pomo Tab does not do

  • It does not sell, share or transfer your data to anyone.
  • It does not use your data for advertising, credit checks or any purpose unrelated to showing it to you.
  • It does not read the websites you visit or your browsing history.
  • It does not load or run code from the internet. Focus sounds are generated inside the extension.

Google user data

Pomo Tab's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It requests read-only access to your calendars, uses it only to display today's events to you, and keeps the access token in your browser. You can disconnect at any time in Pomo settings, or remove access from your Google Account under Security, Third-party connections.

Your choices

  • Every connection is optional. Leave Todoist, Google or calendar addresses blank and those parts stay empty.
  • Deny location and type a city instead, or leave both blank.
  • Disconnect Google or clear your Todoist token in settings, or remove the extension to delete everything it stored.
